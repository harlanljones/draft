# D-002: Empirical Outcome Proposal

Status: constrained proposal; not authorized for model-quality claims.

For an all-2015-2023 feasibility pilot using pinned SABR Lahman 2025, define the primary target as
**MLB debut within two complete post-draft seasons, excluding the draft-year partial season**. This
is observable for every requested class through the completed 2025 season.

Longer completed-season sensitivity cohorts are:

| Horizon | Eligible cohorts with Lahman 2025 |
|---|---|
| Three seasons | 2015-2022 |
| Five seasons | 2015-2020 |
| Seven seasons | 2015-2018 |

Secondary role-specific outcomes may use documented Lahman MLB counting statistics over the same
horizon. They must not be called WAR. Lahman does not support complete minor-league performance,
affiliated-ball participation, signing, bonus, or authoritative WAR outcomes.

The two-season target prioritizes common cohort maturity over baseball completeness. Domain review
must decide whether it is useful enough to justify an empirical pilot. A lawful cohort roster and
predictor source remain prerequisites; Lahman cannot supply either.
