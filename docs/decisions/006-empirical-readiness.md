# D-006: Empirical Readiness Decision

Status: **no-go for the proposed full 2015-2023 empirical backtest as of 2026-09-17**.

The proposed stack does not currently provide a lawful, reproducible left-hand draft cohort or
authorized pre-draft amateur predictors. Open Lahman outcomes and Chadwick identity infrastructure
cannot repair that absence: using either as the cohort would select on professional survival and
erase the negative class.

A limited empirical pilot may start only when all four gates in `docs/sources/registry.md` pass.
Coverage must then be measured by year, role, field, missingness, timestamp, and exclusion. Failed
coverage or identity thresholds narrow the claim or stop the run; current pages and inferred dates
cannot backfill gaps.

The preferred acquisition design is now two contracts: an MLB/MLBAM publisher-supplied 2015-2023
draft-selection cohort plus 6-4-3 Charts predictors, with Sports Info Solutions evaluated for
2015-2016 and coverage gaps. This is a negotiation path, not approved access. Conference/university
exports are a fragmentation pilot; TrackMan and Prep Baseball are supplemental rather than cohort
solutions. If historical negotiations fail, begin a consented, preregistered 2026-forward multi-site
dataset rather than scraping mutable pages.

Empirical benchmark scoring remains independently sealed. If authorized files arrive, MLB Pipeline
is the proposed primary ordinal benchmark because dated list editions exist across 2015-2023.
FanGraphs is secondary and tier-first because THE BOARD launched in 2018 and older tabs can be
retroactive. Every eligible payload requires permission, edition time, checksum, semantics, identity
audit, and common-universe overlap reporting.

See `reports/Baseball source feasibility.md` and `reports/Open amateur baseball data.md` for evidence
and citations.
